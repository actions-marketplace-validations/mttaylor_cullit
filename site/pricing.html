Frequently asked questions

What does BYOK mean?

Bring Your Own Key. You provide your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or Ollama. Cullit never stores or proxies your keys — they stay in your environment.

Can I use Cullit without AI?

Yes. Set your provider to none to use template-based generation from your git history. No API key required. But the free tier also includes 3 AI generations per month — bring your own key and try it.

What counts as a “generation”?

Each run of cullit generate counts as one generation, whether via CLI, GitHub Action, or API.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from the billing dashboard or email sales@cullit.io. Access continues through your billing period; partial periods are not prorated.

What is your refund policy?

Fees are non-refundable except where required by applicable law. Full policy details are in Terms.

How do I recover a lost API key?

Your CULLIT_API_KEY is always visible in the Billing tab of your dashboard. If you are locked out, email support@cullit.io and we will verify ownership and resend it.

How do I sign in?

Click Sign in on the dashboard. You can use GitHub, GitLab, Google, or email — whatever works best for your team. No separate password is required for social logins.

Why limit the free tier?

AI generation costs real compute. Pro access covers those costs and funds continued development. The free tier gives you 3 generations per month — enough to evaluate Cullit on a real project. When you’re ready for production use, upgrade from the dashboard and pick the Pro plan that matches your current seat count.

How do seats work on the Pro plan?

The Pro plan uses simple seat-based billing at $9/seat/month ($8.10/seat on annual). Start with 1 seat and add more anytime from the billing dashboard. All features are unlocked regardless of seat count.

How does Pro pricing scale?

Pro billing is $9/seat/month ($8.10/seat on annual). No minimum seats required. Limits scale with your team: 500 base generations plus 100 per seat, and 100 base projects plus 5 per seat. Add or remove seats anytime from the billing dashboard.
